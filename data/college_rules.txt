College Library Rules

Students can borrow up to three books at a time.

Books should be returned within fourteen days.

Late returns will result in a fine.

----------------------------------------

Hostel Rules

Students must return to the hostel before 9 PM.

Visitors are allowed only between 10 AM and 6 PM.

----------------------------------------

Exam Rules

Students should carry their ID card.

Electronic gadgets are not allowed inside the examination hall.

Attendance below 75% makes a student ineligible for exams.
